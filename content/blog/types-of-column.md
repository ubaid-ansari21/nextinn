Types of columns

1. Square column or Rectangular column.

2. Circular column.

3. ''L'' type column.

4. "T" type column.

5. "V" type column.

6. Hexagon type column.

7. Arch type column.

8. "Y" type column.

1.  **Square column**:  
    Square or rectangular columns are generally used in construction of buildings, which are common in practice, these types of column are provided only if the shape of room is rectangular or square.


2.  **Circular column**:
    Circular column are special designed columns, they are generally used in piling and in the elevation designs of a building structures.


3.  **"L" Type of column**:
    L - types of columns are generally used in the corner of the plot boundary of the building structure.


4.  **"T" type of column**:
    T-types of columns are used depending on design requirement and also in over bridges, etc.


5.  **"V" Type column**:
    Depending upon the structure, they are also constructed in buildings, if the shape of the room is trapezoidal.


6.  **Hexagon type column**:
    Hexagon columns are generally modified columns to give a good look to the column, which is provided in open verandah and halls.


7.  **Arch type column**:
    These types of columns are designed if the room has arch shape.


8.  **"Y" type column**:
    These types of columns are generally used in the construction of bridges, flyovers etc.
